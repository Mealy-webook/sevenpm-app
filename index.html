sevenpm

The app,
in your browser

This is the real SEVENPM app prototype — the same React Native code that runs on iPhone, compiled for the web. Click and drag inside the phone exactly as you would on a device.

Worth a look

  1. Onboarding — swipe the three cards, or Skip.
  2. Discover — the festival rail and the vinyl event page.
  3. Select tickets — steppers, extras, checkout.
  4. Bookings → Tickets — the QR pass, Sell and Send.
  5. Send a ticket — by link or by email, then slide to confirm.
  6. Wallet — balance, cashless card, transactions.
  7. Resale — listings to buy, and your own listings' status.
